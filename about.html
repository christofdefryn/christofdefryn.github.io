About me

Background & Interests

Christof Defryn is a Business Engineer (2012) and obtained a PhD in Applied Economics at the University of Antwerp (2017). From 2017 to 2023, he was Assistant Professor of Operations Research at the Maastricht University School of Business and Economics (NL), an institution renowned for its problem-based learning approach. Since 2023, he has been appointed as Associate Professor of Operations Research and Supply Chain Management at the Faculty of Business and Economics of the University of Antwerp. He is a member of the Antwerp Operations Research Group ANT/OR and leads multiple research lines on the development and application of optimisation models and algorithms for logistics and supply chain management problems.
